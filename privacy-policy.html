Words in Hiding — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Who operates this game

Words in Hiding is a word-search game operated by Graham Lamb. You can reach us at wordsinhiding@gmail.com.

What the game collects

The game itself collects no personal information. There are no user accounts in the game, and the game does not ask for your name, email address, location, or contacts. We do not sell personal information, and the game contains no advertising trackers.

Settings stored on your device

The game remembers your choices — puzzle size, direction mix, and language — in your browser's local storage, on your own device only. These settings never leave your device and are not sent to us. You can erase them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

The Facebook version

When you play Words in Hiding inside Facebook or Messenger (a Facebook Instant Game), Meta Platforms, Inc. provides the platform the game runs on and may process information about you under Meta's own privacy policy. The game receives only your language setting from Facebook, so it can open in your language. The game does not receive your Facebook profile, your friend list, or your photos.

The website version

The version of the game on our own website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that counts visits in aggregate without cookies and without collecting personal information. The Facebook version does not use any analytics service.

Children

Words in Hiding is a family game suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Words in Hiding keeps no account and stores no copy of your information on any server, so there is nothing held by us to delete. The only data connected with the game is the settings stored on your own device (see above). To delete them, clear the site or app data for Words in Hiding in your browser or device settings; your game login is forgotten when you press Close in the game or close the tab. If you believe we hold any information about you, email wordsinhiding@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: wordsinhiding@gmail.com.
