Words in Hiding — Help & FAQ

What is Words in Hiding?

Words in Hiding is a word-search puzzle game. Every puzzle is built around a theme — music, animals, cooking, space, and 140 more — and the words are hidden in a grid of letters for you to find. When you have found them all, the letters left over spell a hidden message about the theme. It is a family game, made by Graham Lamb.

The game asks me for a password. What is that?

Words in Hiding on this website is for friends. The first screen says FRIENDS ONLY and asks for one password — there is no username, no account, and nothing to sign up for. If a friend sent you the game, they can give you the password; if you arrived another way, email us at wordsinhiding@gmail.com. Entering the password opens the game for that visit; pressing Close in the game, or closing the tab, logs you out again.

Which browsers and devices work?

Edge, Chrome, Opera, Firefox, or Safari, on a phone, a tablet, or a computer. (Opera Mini cannot run the game.) On Facebook and Messenger, the game plays inside the Facebook app.

What puzzle sizes are there?

Three sizes: 12×12, 15×15, and 20×20. Every device can play 12×12. Tablets and larger screens also offer 15×15. The 20×20 size needs a display at least 1920 pixels wide, so it appears on larger monitors. The choice is in Setup, which you can reopen at any time.

I have two screens, but 20×20 does not appear. Why?

Your computer is probably set to show the same picture on both screens (mirroring). When screens are mirrored, the computer reports the smaller screen's size, and 20×20 stays hidden. Set your displays to extend instead, so each screen has its own desktop:

Windows: Settings → System → Display → "Extend these displays" (not Duplicate).
macOS: System Settings → Displays → select the second screen → "Use as: Extended display" (not Mirror).
Linux: Settings → Displays → "Join Displays" (not Mirror).

Then reopen the game — or reopen Setup — and 20×20 will be offered.

Which directions can words run in?

All eight: right, left, up, down, and the four diagonals — and backwards words are included in whichever directions you allow. In Setup, open Directions to set your own mix: one percentage for each direction, always totalling 100. The game builds each puzzle toward your mix, and the actual mix achieved is shown under the status line while you play. The standard mix is Right 20, Down 20, Left 10, Up 10, and 10 for each diagonal.

Which languages can I play in?

Five: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, and Latin. On your first visit the game opens in your browser's language if it is one of the first four; otherwise it opens in English. You can change the language at any time in Setup, under Language — the whole game follows, controls, messages, and theme names included — and your choice is remembered on your device.

What are Find and Auto Play?

Helpers. Find shows you where one word is hiding, for when you are truly stuck. Auto Play solves the whole puzzle for you. They are there for youngsters who are learning, and for anyone who wants to see how a puzzle comes out — the game itself is meant to be solved by eye, word by word.

Can I print a puzzle?

Yes. The Print button prints the current puzzle on one page, with the words you have already found greyed out.

What does the game store about me?

Nothing on any server. Your settings — size, direction mix, and language — are kept in your browser, on your own device, and never leave it. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
